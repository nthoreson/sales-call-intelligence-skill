# Sales Call Intelligence — Claude Skill

Your prospects are already telling you exactly what to say in your marketing. Most companies ignore it. This skill captures it.

Upload your sales call transcripts and get back a structured intelligence report with specific, ready-to-use marketing content: social posts, website copy, FAQ additions, lead magnet ideas, email drafts, and ad concepts — all written in your prospect's actual language.

---

## What It Does

1. Reads one or more sales call transcripts (text, PDF, or pasted)
2. Extracts questions, objections, and aha moments from each call
3. Identifies patterns across calls (frequency = signal strength)
4. Outputs a full marketing intelligence report
5. Generates six ready-to-use marketing deliverables based on what it found

Works with a single call or a batch. The more calls you feed it, the stronger the pattern recognition.

Compatible with exports from Otter.ai, Fireflies, Gong, Fathom, tl;dv, and any transcript format.

---

## Installation

**Option 1 — Install the .skill file directly**

1. Download `sales-call-intelligence.skill` from this repo
2. Open Claude Code
3. Run `/install-skill` and select the downloaded file

**Option 2 — Manual install**

1. Copy `SKILL.md` from this repo
2. Save it to `~/.claude/skills/sales-call-intelligence/SKILL.md`
3. Restart Claude Code

---

## How to Use It

Once installed, type `/sales-call-intelligence` in Claude Code and upload your transcript files when prompted. Claude will handle the rest.

You can also trigger it by mentioning "analyze sales calls," "turn this transcript into marketing," or uploading a transcript file directly.

---

## What You Get Back

**Intelligence extraction**
- Every question asked (with call frequency and what it signals)
- Every objection raised (categorized by type, with the real concern underneath)
- Every aha moment identified (with what triggered it and why it worked)
- Pattern summary across calls (the top messaging gaps and conversion blockers)

**Six marketing deliverables**
- LinkedIn/social post draft
- Website copy update (specific page, specific section, specific words)
- FAQ additions
- Lead magnet concept
- Email/newsletter draft
- Ad concept with copy

All written in your prospect's language, not polished marketing language.

---

## Why This Approach Works

Questions asked early in calls reveal messaging gaps. Objections reveal the real fears your marketing needs to address. Aha moments are your best headline candidates — whatever breaks through skepticism on a call will break through it in an ad.

The skill preserves raw prospect language. "I didn't realize you could do that without us having to rebuild everything" is more useful than "ease of implementation." That's the language that converts.
